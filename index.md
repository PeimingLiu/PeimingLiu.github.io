Hi! I am a **Senior Staff Compiler Engineer** at [Modular](https://www.modular.com/),
now part of [Qualcomm](https://www.qualcomm.com/). I work on the graph compiler and
the [Mojo](https://www.modular.com/mojo) programming language, so that our stack
can compile and run ML models with state-of-the-art performance.

Before that, I was a software engineer at Google Research, where I was a key
contributor to the [MLIR sparse tensor dialect](https://mlir.llvm.org/docs/Dialects/SparseTensorOps/)
(the MLIR Sparsifier).

I received my Ph.D. from the
[Computer Science & Engineering Department](https://engineering.tamu.edu/cse/index.html) at
[Texas A&M University](https://www.tamu.edu/), advised by
[Prof. Jeff Huang](https://parasol.tamu.edu/~jeff/), working on programming languages
and static program analysis. I obtained my Bachelor's degree from
[Huazhong University of Science and Technology](http://english.hust.edu.cn/).

You can find my [CV here](https://peimingliu.github.io/asset/pic/CV.pdf).
